# ADR-007: v0.1 Scope Contract

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-09

## Context

A candidate standard for institutional commitments can overclaim legal authority or demonstrated interoperability, or become so qualified that its intended contribution is no longer clear. The scope of v0.1 therefore requires one authoritative statement.

## Decision

Adopt the v0.1 scope contract published at `05-public-review/v0.1-scope-contract.md` as the authoritative statement of what ICSL v0.1 claims and does not claim.

In summary, v0.1:

- defines protocol, package, and receipt semantics for review;
- does not certify the legal correctness of any encoding;
- does not prove production interoperability;
- treats receipts as structured, rebuttable evidence, not legal authority;
- supports deterministic evaluation only where the encoded protocol is determinate — discretion must be represented as discretion;
- requires conformance claims to name class, ICSL version, and test-suite version;
- never allows an encoding to override the underlying legal or institutional source.

## Consequences

- Any statement in the corpus or in public communication that conflicts with the scope contract is a defect.
- The scope contract complements, and is enforced alongside, the claim guardrails in `05-public-review/claim-guardrails.md`.
- Widening the scope requires a new ADR.
