# ADR-001: Name Expansion of ICSL

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-09

## Context

Early documents used two expansions of the acronym ICSL: "Coordination and Service Language" and "Institutional Commitment Specification Language." A standard cannot carry two names for itself.

The model is centred on the CommitmentPoint: protocols specify the commitments an institution makes and how they are gated, evidenced, and discharged. "Commitment Specification" describes the model; "Coordination and Service" does not.

## Decision

ICSL expands to "Institutional Commitment Specification Language" everywhere.

The former "Coordination and Service Language" expansion is deprecated.

## Consequences

- All documents, schemas, and public statements use a single expansion.
- Any surviving occurrence of the old expansion is a defect and should be reported.
- No change to the acronym itself, machine identifiers, or version tokens.
